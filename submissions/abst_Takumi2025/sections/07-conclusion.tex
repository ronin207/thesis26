\section{Conclusion}
We formalized the field-mismatch tax and a criterion for when a custom precompile is worth building. We ran the first post-quantum anonymous credential inside a zkVM, within a personal machine's $24\,\mathrm{GB}$ budget. And we found, with SP1's shipped tools, that no single proof both resists a quantum computer and hides its data. Closing this gap is left to future work. One route is to make the post-quantum proof hide its own data with a hash-based method~\cite{cryptoeprint:2024/1037,cryptoeprint:2026/683}, rather than the elliptic-curve wrap that brings the quantum weakness back.
