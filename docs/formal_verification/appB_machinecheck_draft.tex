\subsection{Machine-checked evidence for the limb gadget}\label{app:machine-check}

The per-row layer rests on the limb-gadget congruence
(Lemma~\ref{lem:limb-gadget}) and, for determinism of the output lanes, on the
canonicality step that the byte-wise less-than cells (\texttt{FieldLtCols})
discharge in Lemma~\ref{lem:round-fn} and Lemma~\ref{lem:compose}. We report a
machine-checked corroboration of exactly that seam. Using native
finite-field and bit-vector reasoning (cvc5 1.3.4 and z3 4.15.4), we encoded a
single \texttt{FieldOpCols} cell over the deployed base field $q$ (KoalaBear,
$q=2^{31}-2^{24}+1$) as its coefficient-wise vanishing identity and asked
whether the identity determines the result cell. The encoding is validated two
ways before any determinism query is read: an oracle query confirms the honest
witness produced by the reference generator
(\texttt{FieldOpCols::populate\_carry\_and\_witness}) satisfies the constraints
(satisfiable), and a negative control that perturbs one result byte is rejected
by both solvers (unsatisfiable). The constraints therefore admit the intended
witness and reject a wrong one, so the check has teeth.

Under that validated encoding the bare cell is \emph{under-constrained}: the
determinism query is satisfiable, with an explicit pair of distinct witnesses on
the same operands. At a small two-limb instance ($n=2$, with a representative
modulus $M$ standing in for $p$) the solver returns operands $a=63{,}751$,
$b=21{,}137$ and two results, the honest $c=1$ and the alternate $c'=c+M$ (with
carries $h$ and $h-1$), both satisfying the vanishing identity coefficient-wise.
This is the same phenomenon Lemma~\ref{lem:limb-gadget} already flags in its own
terms, and it is structural rather than an artifact of the small instance: the
gadget pins the result only within its residue class modulo the operative
modulus, not as a canonical integer. Adding the result-canonicality constraint $c<p$, the check
the chip realises through its \texttt{FieldLtCols} cells
(\texttt{rc\_add\_range\_check}, ``B-8''; \texttt{sbox1\_cube\_range\_check} and
\texttt{quad\_mul\_2/3\_range\_check}, ``F1''), makes the determinism query
unsatisfiable on both solvers. The harness thus independently rediscovered, from
the constraint set alone, that output determinism requires the canonicality
check that Lemma~\ref{lem:round-fn} and Lemma~\ref{lem:compose} name, and
exhibited the concrete failure without it. This strengthens the evidence for the
per-row layer's range-and-canonicality side-conditions; it does not close
Assumption~\ref{asm:air}.

\paragraph{Scope of the check.} The result is confined to the base-case
arithmetic gadget, the \texttt{FieldOpCols} cell that the seven per-row
constraint families are built from, checked at small operand width ($n=2$ base-$256$
limbs) over the deployed base field $q$. It does not machine-check the full
$3{,}819$-column row, the $26$ field-operation cells composed within it, or the
ten families as one query: a monolithic bit-blasted encoding does not reach that
size, with the determinism (unsatisfiable) direction tractable only to
$n\approx2$--$3$ and the detection (satisfiable) direction to $n\approx8$, against
a full-row width of $n=32$. Closing the full row by machine is future work and
follows a decomposition, not brute force: per-gadget-type determinism at small
representative width with the canonicality side-conditions (this check), an
algebraic $n$-uniformity argument that the constraint shape and the
canonical-residue and monic-division uniqueness are independent of the limb
count, and a cell-composition induction that a row of deterministic cells with
canonicalised outputs is deterministic. The three lookup-borne families (round
count, memory binding, row scheduling) lie outside this arithmetic check and
remain carried by Assumption~\ref{asm:lookup}, as does the code-to-constraint
transcription fidelity of Section~\ref{app:status}. Assumption~\ref{asm:air} is
therefore \emph{strengthened at the base-case per-row layer, not closed}: the
check clears the prerequisite that a faithful, validated encoding of the
gadget's constraints answers the determinism question and confirms the
canonicality dependency the thesis already routes through
Lemma~\ref{lem:round-fn} and Lemma~\ref{lem:compose}, while the full-row,
seven-family, and lookup-borne obligations stand as before.
